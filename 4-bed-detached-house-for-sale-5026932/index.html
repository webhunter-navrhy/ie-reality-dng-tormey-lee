Set on an impressive c. 0.71-acre site with mature gardens, No. 2 Hillside is a fabulous four-bedroom detached family home offering an exceptional combination of space, style and tranquillity in the charming village of Clonalvy. Built in 2003 with a C1 BER rating, this stunning family home enjoys a peaceful countryside setting while remaining within easy access of Dublin and the surrounding towns.

Clonalvy itself offers a lifestyle that is becoming increasingly sought after. The village has a charming and peaceful atmosphere together with a strong sense of community, making it particularly appealing to those looking to enjoy a quieter pace of life. A number of new homes have recently been built locally, further increasing the area's appeal to families seeking the benefits of a country lifestyle. The historic and scenic surroundings, combined with the close-knit community give Clonalvy a distinctive character.

Approached through a gated entrance, this property features a swooping tarmac driveway, mature gardens which extend to both the front and the rear of the property, with access to a detached garage. With a farm to the rear, the property enjoys stunning views over the surrounding countryside and a wonderful sense of openness. There are two dedicated outdoor seating areas: a decked area to the rear, ideal for outdoor dining and entertaining, together with a smaller paved sitting area to the side of the house, providing another peaceful spot from which to enjoy the gardens and rural surroundings.

Internally, the house is wonderfully spacious and stylish. The accommodation opens into a beautiful central entrance hallway, with the staircase positioned as a focal point at the heart of the home. The kitchen and dining area boasts a central island creating an attractive focal point to the kitchen and French doors leading out to the garden. There is a large utility room, providing valuable additional storage and practical working space.

There are two reception rooms. The front living room is bright and welcoming with a feature fireplace and double doors that lead directly into the kitchen. The larger reception room at the other side of the house enjoys French doors opening onto the garden, creating a fantastic space for relaxing and entertaining. A separate WC is also conveniently positioned off the central hallway.

Upstairs, the accommodation comprises of four generously sized bedrooms and two bathrooms. The main bedroom has its own walk-in wardrobe and en-suite, while the remaining bedrooms are all spacious and bright. The main bathroom is beautifully finished and features a corner bath, creating a lovely space to relax and unwind.

Further practical features include a water softener system and a CCTV camera system which can be accessed remotely by phone, adding both convenience and security.

No. 2 Hillside is a wonderful family home in a peaceful countryside setting, combining spacious interiors with mature gardens and stunning rural views. Offering the best of country living while remaining within easy reach of Dublin and surrounding towns, this is a home with plenty to offer families seeking space, comfort and a quieter pace of life.
